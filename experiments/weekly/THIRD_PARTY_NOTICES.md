This mockup introduces deterministic sample commit data, calendar alignment, weekly height mapping, active-day pine counts, dark/light palettes, and the README composition. All commit data is fictional. Distant background washes, moon, paper grain and shoreline are decorative; weekly foreground mountains and pine counts encode the sample activity.
