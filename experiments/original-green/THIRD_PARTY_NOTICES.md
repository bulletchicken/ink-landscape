# Attribution and third-party notices

The mountain contour and tapered brush techniques in this edition are adapted from **{Shan, Shui}*** by **Lingdong Huang**:

- [Original repository](https://github.com/LingDong-/shan-shui-inf)
- Original implementation: `index.html`, particularly `Mount.mountain` and `stroke`.
- Original artwork and algorithms: © 2018 Lingdong Huang.
- License: MIT, reproduced below.

This edition continues the first design’s adapted mountain and brush approach. The separate Version 2 executes Lingdong’s original drawing engine directly. This edition adds monthly commit mapping, attached date labels, a dark palette, parallax, sparse varied vegetation, clouds, water motion, and fish animation around the simplified artwork.

All activity data is fictional. Main mountain heights encode monthly activity. Trees, background terrain, clouds, water, and fish are decorative and do not represent people, active days, or additional commits.
